# Glowvale Skincare Brand Guidelines (2026 Edition)
**Source ID**: S-01  
**Owner**: Brand Manager  
**Effective Date**: 2026-11-01  
**Next Review**: 2027-05-01  

Welcome to the official Glowvale content and compliance manual. Every copywriter, marketing manager, freelancer, and AI tool must adhere strictly to these rules before any marketing material, social post, ad, or packaging goes public.

---

## Section 1: Brand Voice & Ethical Tone

### Rule: Tone 1.1 - Brand Demeanor
- Glowvale communicates with scientific honesty, warmth, and grounded optimism.
- Never use cheap, sensationalized language like "cheapest skincare", "unbelievable steal", or spammy tropes that degrade brand perception.
- Recommended phrasing: "accessible daily skincare", "formulated for everyday radiance", "exceptional value".

### Rule: Tone 1.2 - Inclusivity and Anti-Colourism
- Glowvale celebrates healthy, hydrated skin of every tone.
- Strictly prohibited: Any reference to skin lightening, "fairness", "gora nikhaar", "instant whitening", or implying dark skin requires correction.
- Approved vocabulary: "radiant", "glowing", "smooth texture", "deep hydration", "khilta nikhaar".

### Rule: Tone 1.3 - Non-Aggressive Sales Language
- Do not create false panic or hyper-aggressive urgency.
- Strictly prohibited: Excessive uppercase words (e.g. "HURRY BUY NOW"), multiple consecutive exclamation points (e.g. "!!!"), or aggressive countdowns.
- Approved alternatives: "Available now", "Discover your routine", "Limited holiday quantities available".

## Section 3: Claims, Evidence & Scientific Integrity

### Rule: Claims 3.1 - Scientific and Clinical Substantiation
- Every efficacy claim must cite an active clinical study or official Product Facts Sheet.
- "Dermatologist-tested on 30 volunteers" is approved ONLY because of Study DT-2026-04 (Source S-05).
- "Clinically proven" is STRICTLY PROHIBITED for any Glowvale cosmetic product without a peer-reviewed double-blind trial on file.
- "Suitable for sensitive skin": currently has NO completed study on file. It must be flagged as **"Unverified, needs source"**. The safe alternative is: "Gentle, fragrance-free formula".

### Rule: Claims 3.2 - Prohibited Efficacy & Medical Claims
- Do not make medical, curative, or miraculous claims.
- Strictly prohibited terms: "cures acne", "treats eczema", "removes wrinkles in days", "miracle", "100% natural", "chemical-free", "instant results".
- Approved alternatives: "helps skin look smoother", "plant-derived ingredients", "supports skin barrier".

### Rule: Claims 3.3 - Third-Party Certifications & Audit Currency
- All certifications (Cruelty-Free, Vegan, Organic) must have a current, unexpired certificate on file.
- Notice: The Cruelty-Free Certification (Source S-06) expired on 2026-10-01 and is pending audit renewal. Any claim stating "Cruelty-free certified" must be held until the Quality Team uploads the renewed certificate.

## Section 4: Naming & Trademark Shield

### Rule: Naming 4.1 - Approved Product Portfolio & Rebranded Assets
- Products must only be named by their approved registered title:
  - "Hydra Glow Serum" (Never "Night Renew Cream" - retired in Q3 2026)
  - "Daily Shield Sunscreen"
  - "Gentle Foam Cleanser"
- Trademark Collision Prevention: Check for confusing similarity with competitor trademarks (Source S-09, e.g. "Dewlite", "GlowBoost", "YouthShield"). When a resemblance is found, issue a warning accompanied by the required legal disclaimer:
  *"Heads up: this sounds similar to [Brand X]. This is an AI suggestion, not legal advice. Please consult your legal team."*
