The problem

One campaign. Many people. One consistent brand.

Marketing content can be created by employees, agencies and AI tools across multiple channels. When teams work from different documents or outdated information, drafts can contain inconsistent terminology, unapproved claims, incorrect prices or conflicting campaign details. Manual review can make these issues difficult to catch consistently.

TrueVoice brings approved brand information and pre-publication checks into one review workflow, helping teams identify potential issues before they publish.

Core capabilities

Review the details that protect brand trust.

Brand rules and claims

Check draft language against configured guidelines, restricted terminology and approved claims.

Pricing and offers

Compare prices and promotional statements with the approved information maintained by the team.

Campaign consistency

Surface potential schedule and cross-channel inconsistencies that need a reviewer's attention.

Protected-name warnings

Identify name-similarity concerns for further review. These warnings are not legal advice.

Knowledge freshness

Help reviewers notice source information that may need re-verification before it is relied upon.

Human approval trail

Support reviewer decisions, draft history and approval verification while keeping people in control.

The workflow

From draft to reviewed content.

1. Maintain

Keep approved facts, prices, offers, terms and rules in the knowledge library.

2. Submit

Send a draft through the workspace for pre-publication checks.

3. Review

Inspect findings and their context, then decide what needs correction.

4. Approve

Record the human review decision and use available approval-verification features.

Frequently asked questions

About TrueVoice

What is TrueVoice?

TrueVoice is a brand-governance and content-review workspace designed to help teams check draft content against approved information before publication.

Does TrueVoice publish content automatically?

No. The product is designed around pre-publication review. Human reviewers remain responsible for the final decision.

Are trademark similarity warnings legal advice?

No. Similarity warnings are prompts for further review and should not replace advice from a qualified legal professional.

Who is TrueVoice for?

It is designed for content creators, marketing teams, agencies, brand reviewers and administrators who maintain approved brand information.
